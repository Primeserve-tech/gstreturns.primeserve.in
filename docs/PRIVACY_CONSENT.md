# One-time purpose-specific privacy consent

Registration includes an unticked checkbox linked to /privacy-notice. Backend requires consent=true and current notice version, binds version into the protected OTP registration payload, and records consent only when registration is verified. No existing customer is marked consented by migration.

Existing customers without consent are redirected from GST Services to My Account → Privacy & Consent once. After granting, backend persists purpose, version, exact wording and timestamp; no repeat tick at login or download. Browser storage is not relied upon. Returning users are prompted only when consent is absent/withdrawn or a new purpose/version explicitly requires renewed consent. Avoid bumping the consent version for cosmetic edits.

Withdrawal is available from My Account with a clear confirmation. Account, balances, refund access and financial records remain. Saved GST connections are removed; all GST controller endpoints require active consent. Worker cancels uninitiated queued requests and releases their reservations, and pauses further polling. Already dispatched upstream requests cannot be recalled. Consent and financial records remain separate from download retention. New processing purposes need a new notice and fresh consent; do not silently broaden wording. Account cancellation/erasure requests are handled through support@primeserve.in rather than a misleading immediate deletion promise.

Migration customer-v9.sql adds customer_consents and append-only consent_events. No secrets stored in consent evidence. Existing authentication and CSRF protect grant/withdraw. Generic account and security administration still works after withdrawal. Tests/privacy_consent.php and tests/run_customer_http.php validate explicit registration, version matching, idempotence, withdrawal, service denial and re-consent. Test mail delivery is injected; no production consent or accounts changed. Rollback must retain consent evidence and the consent enforcement code; do not revert to a build that ignores withdrawn consent. Review notice/legal retention with the business before production deployment.
